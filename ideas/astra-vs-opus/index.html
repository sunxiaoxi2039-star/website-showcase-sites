20 道 Astra 原题，一字不改，

从公开仓库挑出 20 件带原始 prompt 的 Astra 作品，每道题交给一个全新上下文的 Opus 5.5 子代理，禁止查看原作。两边用同一脚本、同一视口截图，再由看不到出处的评审代理按五项各 10 分打分，抽 5 道对调 A/B 换评委复核。 We picked 20 public Astra works that ship with their original prompts and gave each prompt to a fresh-context Opus 5.5 subagent that was forbidden to look at the original. Both sides were screenshotted by the same script at the same viewport, then scored by a judge agent that couldn’t see who made what — five criteria, 10 points each. Five pairs were re-judged with A/B swapped by a new judge.

最有参考价值The ones that matter

重量级 3 题：差距只有 3–5.5 分Heavyweights: only 3–5.5 points apart

这三件 Astra 原作是单独打磨的游戏（苔翼 One Shot + Xhigh 用时 21 分钟；瓜体实验室 5 MB；雷霆战机多轮迭代 + 协作审阅）。5.5 三题全胜，但 Astra 在界面成品感上更强——暂停、静音、难度选择、关间强化；5.5 更抓住了题目「那个味儿」，也有实打实的 bug。瓜体实验室换标签复评 42 : 43.5，基本打平。These three Astra originals are individually polished games (Mosswing: one shot at Xhigh, 21 minutes; Melon Lab: 5 MB; Thunderfall: several iterations with collaborative review). 5.5 won all three, but Astra’s interfaces feel more finished — pause, mute, difficulty select, between-stage upgrades — while 5.5 caught the spirit of each prompt better and shipped real bugs of its own. Melon Lab’s swapped-label recheck came out 42–43.5: essentially a tie.

投入悬殊Lopsided effort

批量组 17 题：比的是投入Batch group of 17: a contest of effort

这 17 件 Astra 原作出自「一次生成 100 件」的合集，单件只有 2–5 KB；5.5 每题独占一个代理，自己截图、改 4–10 轮，文件 40–340 KB。等于拿随堂作文对单篇精修——这组分差说明的是「投入多一个数量级能做到什么」，不能直接读成模型差距。These 17 Astra originals come from a set of 100 generated in one go — 2–5 KB each. On the 5.5 side every prompt got its own agent that screenshotted and revised its work 4–10 times, producing 40–340 KB files. It’s a timed classroom essay against a polished single piece: the gap shows what an order of magnitude more effort buys, not the gap between models.

偏差无法排除Unremovable bias

评委也是 ClaudeThe judge is Claude too

没有第三方模型可用，盲评代理同样是 Opus 5.5。A/B 匿名、署名抹掉，换标签复核也只能排除「偏好 A 位 / B 位」，排除不了「偏好自家风格」。分数请当参考——最可靠的是你自己点开两边玩一下。No third-party model was available, so the blind judge was Opus 5.5 as well. A/B labels were anonymous and signatures scrubbed, but label-swapping only rules out position bias — not a taste for its own style. Treat the scores as a reference; the most reliable test is opening both and playing them yourself.

全 X 网搜来的 6 件，

2026 年 9 月 3 日 GPT-6 Astra 发布后的一周，X 上涌出一批「一句话生成一个能跑的 3D 网页」的作品。从中挑了 6 件，用 Claude Opus 5 各自重写一遍——左边是原作出处，右边是临摹版本，点「内嵌预览」就能在本页跑。 In the week after GPT-6 Astra launched on 3 September 2026, X filled up with “one sentence, one working 3D web page” demos. We picked six and had Claude Opus 5 rewrite each one — the original’s source on one side, the copy on the other. Tap “Live preview” to run it right here.

先说清楚：这是「按描述重写」，不是 1:1 像素复刻。To be clear: these are rewrites from descriptions, not pixel-perfect replicas.

做这批临摹时，运行环境的出口网络屏蔽了 x.com 与各 demo 域名，没法逐帧对着原作改。每件临摹依据的是原帖文字与社区整理（awesome-gpt-6-astra）里的作品说明，代码全部重写。所以每张卡片都带着原作链接：请点开原作自己对照；「自评还原度」只是对自己实现完成度的判断，不是对原作的评分。While making these, the sandbox’s outbound network blocked x.com and every demo domain, so there was no way to tune against the originals frame by frame. Each copy is based on the post text and the community write-ups in awesome-gpt-6-astra, with all code written from scratch. That’s why every card links the original: please open it and compare for yourself. The “self-rated fidelity” is a judgement of how complete my own implementation is, not a score for the original.

临摹下来的三点观察Three takeaways from copying

难的不是渲染，是编排The hard part is choreography, not rendering

这批作品真正的设计工作，是把一个题材拆成两三个可操作的开关：深度、冲程、昼夜、时间轴。几何和着色器都是熟活，而「给观众什么把手」决定了它是 demo 还是玩具。The real design work in these pieces is breaking a subject into two or three controls you can grab: depth, stroke, day and night, a timeline. Geometry and shaders are routine; which handles you give the viewer decides whether it’s a demo or a toy.

程序化几何足够撑场Procedural geometry carries the show

六件临摹没有用任何外部模型或贴图，全部是代码里长出来的盒子、锥体和噪声。观感的差别主要来自配色、雾和动效节奏，而不是资产精度。None of the six copies uses an external model or texture — everything is boxes, cones and noise grown in code. The difference in feel comes mostly from palette, fog and motion rhythm, not asset fidelity.

差距集中在「打磨」The gap is in the polish

和原作最可能拉开的地方是后期与手感：辉光、景深、真实建模、镜头调度。临摹版靠平面着色与克制的调色去补，但那层「被反复调过」的质感确实要靠时间堆。Where the originals most likely pull ahead is post-processing and feel: bloom, depth of field, real models, camera work. The copies compensate with flat shading and restrained colour, but that “tuned over and over” quality really does take time.

方法与公平性Method & fairness

每一轮是怎么做的，
哪里不公平。How each round was run,

R1

临摹 · Opus 5Copies · Opus 5

依据原帖文字与社区整理的说明重写，网络屏蔽了原作；还原度是自评。代码为单文件 HTML + three.js r128，纯程序化几何。Rewritten from post text and community write-ups while the originals were blocked; fidelity is self-rated. Single-file HTML + three.js r128, purely procedural geometry.

R2

独立逆向 · Opus 5.5Re-derivation · Opus 5.5

每件一个全新上下文子代理，看不到第一版任何东西。起跑线不一：梵高与 V8 只有文字；深渊、首尔读过 README；轨道核心读过并跑过原作源码（未复用），另有一处搜索摘要泄漏，已在心得中交代。One fresh-context subagent per work, blind to everything from round 1. Starting lines vary: Van Gogh and V8 had text only; Abyssal and Seoul read the READMEs; Orbital Core read and ran the original source (reusing none), plus one leak via a search snippet, disclosed in its notes.

R3

对打 · Opus 5.5Showdown · Opus 5.5

原 prompt 一字不改（作者追加的反馈也原样给），格式约束同原作；同机同脚本 1400×875、加载 4 秒后截图；匿名 A/B 盲评五项各 10 分，差 ≤ 1 分记平；5 道对调 A/B 换评委复核，全部一致。Original prompts verbatim (including any follow-up feedback from the author), same format constraints; same machine, same script, 1400×875, screenshot 4 s after load; anonymous A/B judging on five 10-point criteria, a gap ≤ 1 counts as a tie; 5 pairs re-judged with labels swapped — all agreed.

  • 投入不对等，偏向 5.5。批量组 17 件是一次生成 100 件中的 2–5 KB 小品；5.5 每题独占一个代理，能自己截图、反复修改。Unequal effort, favouring 5.5. The 17 batch pieces are 2–5 KB sketches from a single 100-piece run; 5.5 had one agent per prompt that could screenshot and revise at will.
  • 评委也是 Claude。匿名与换标签只排除了位置偏好，排除不了自家风格偏好。The judge is Claude too. Anonymity and label swaps rule out position bias, not a preference for its own style.
  • 雷霆战机原作是多轮迭代（协作代理、审阅与造型返工），5.5 拿到原题 + 那条返工反馈，但只做一轮。Thunderfall’s original took several iterations (collaborating agents, reviews and art rework); 5.5 got the prompt plus that rework feedback, but only one pass.
  • 截图是软件渲染。重型 WebGL 在真机上会更流畅，两边吃同一个亏。Screenshots use software rendering. Heavy WebGL runs smoother on real hardware; both sides take the same hit.
  • 代价不低。第三轮单件用时 7–57 分钟不等，雷霆战机一件就用了约 30 万 token。It wasn’t cheap. Round-3 builds took 7–57 minutes each; Thunderfall alone used about 300,000 tokens.
