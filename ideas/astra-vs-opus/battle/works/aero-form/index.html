Aeroform is drawn as three stacked systems that read clearly when pulled apart. Every figure below is imagined for this speculative study — nothing has been built, tested or measured.

01 — Upper

Aero-mesh shell

A single-layer engineered knit: tight cells around the heel for hold, open diamond cells over the forefoot where air should move. The swept fin locks the midfoot without extra layers.
