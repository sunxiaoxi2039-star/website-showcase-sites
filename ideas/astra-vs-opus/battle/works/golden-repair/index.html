Kintsugi · 金継ぎ

Where it broke,
it now holds light.

A tribute
An homage to the Japanese art of mending with gold — offered as a meditation, not a craft tutorial. No techniques are taught here.

I II

I — The break.Eleven lines, one fall. The glaze remembers exactly where it let go.

II — The mend.The same eleven lines, now the brightest thing on the table. Nothing was hidden; the history became the ornament.

金 — kingold Gold does not tarnish, which is why it was trusted to keep a break rather than erase it. The bowl is not returned to what it was. It is returned to use, carrying openly everything that happened to it.
