A note on method

This is an artistic study, not a physics model. No masses are weighed and no torques are solved. The lean you give the beam is a gesture; the lag and sway of the lower arms are composed by eye, the way a line in a drawing answers the one before it.

What it borrows from the midcentury mobile is its temperament: balance that is felt before it is measured, colour set against a quiet wall, and a wire thin enough to disappear.

  • Terracotta ellipse — the counterweight, alone at the end of the long arm.
  • Ocher triangle — the first to answer when the beam turns.
  • Teal semicircle — a shallow bowl held up to the light.
  • Deep olive disc — the full stop at the far tip.
