Model notes

A schematic model

This exhibit evaluates one static equation, Δh = ΔP / (ρg). It is an idealised illustration, not a measurement or a fluid simulation.

  • Water density fixed at 1000 kg/m³ and gravity at 9.81 m/s²; no temperature, salinity or altitude effects.
  • Both limbs share one uniform bore, so each meniscus moves exactly Δh/2 from rest.
  • Surface tension, capillary rise, the weight of air above the water and tube elasticity are ignored.
  • Port B is vented to atmosphere, so all pressures shown are gauge pressures.

The gentle settling of the column is drawn for legibility only; the numbers always show the final equilibrium. Vertical scales are drawn 1 : 1 in millimetres; fittings, stand and dials are schematic.
