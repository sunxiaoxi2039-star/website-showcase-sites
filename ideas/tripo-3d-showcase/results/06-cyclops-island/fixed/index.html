HOW TO PLAY

Escape Polyphemus

  1. Approach the cave. Getting close to the sleeping giant, or sprinting nearby, raises his suspicion. At full suspicion he wakes early.
  2. Take the three supplies. Stand right next to each store and press E. Any theft wakes him.
  3. Hold the jetty. Stay inside the marked circle for 11 seconds while the crew board. Leaving pauses the boarding.

He must close to 3.6 units before striking. Ground Breaker locks where you stood when he raised the club — step out of the orange circle or dodge through it. Every third slam is charged (gold): a shockwave ring expands outward and hurts where its edge passes you. Dodging gives brief invulnerability.
